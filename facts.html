Facts about Bananas

1. Bananas are a type of berry.
2. Bananas grow upwards against gravity, forming their curved shape.
3. Most Cavendish bananas are sterile and grown from cuttings, making every single one a genetic clone.
4. A banana is roughly 75% water.
5. Bananas are slightly radioactive.
6. The scientific name of the banana is Musa Sapientum, which means 'fruit of the wise men.'
7. Some cultures (especially Japan) use the fiber in the banana plant to make fabric and sometimes even paper.
8. Cavendish bananas, the most common type of banana today, was named after the 6th Duke of Devonshire, William George Spencer Cavendish.
9. A single banana is called a finger, a cluster is called a hand, and an entire grouping is called a bunch.
10. Bananas are less dense than water, so they are able to float.
